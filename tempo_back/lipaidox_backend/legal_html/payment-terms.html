Payment Terms

Last updated: September 2026

How paying for content, subscriptions and plans works on Lipaidox.

1. Prices and currency

Prices are set in US dollars (USD). Where we show a Tanzanian shilling (TZS) amount, it is an estimate for your reference; the amount your bank or mobile money provider charges may differ slightly because of their exchange rate.

2. How you pay

Purchases are paid from your Lipaidox wallet. You can add money to your wallet by card, through a secure payment page, or by M-Pesa, through a prompt on your phone.

When you choose Card or M-Pesa at checkout, the amount needed is added to your wallet first and then used for the purchase. Lipaidox never sees or stores your full card number.

3. What you get

  • One-time purchase — permanent access to that content's exclusive material for as long as the content and your account remain on Lipaidox.
  • Rental — access for the period shown before you pay (for example 24 hours), starting at purchase.
  • Subscription — access to a creator's subscriber-only content for each billing period (monthly or yearly). It renews automatically until you cancel; after cancelling, access continues until the end of the period already paid for.

4. Live credits

Creators use live credits to stream Premium Live: 100 credits cost $10 and give 15 minutes of streaming. Credits are deducted for the time streamed and are non-refundable once used.

5. Creator plans and platform fees

Creators choose a plan to monetize. The platform fee is taken from what a creator earns: 30% on Basic, 25% on Go Plus and 20% on Premium. The Free plan cannot sell content. Plans are billed monthly, or yearly at a discount.

6. Failed or pending payments

You are only charged when the payment provider confirms the payment. If a payment fails, nothing is charged and no access is granted. If you were charged but did not receive access, contact us with the payment reference and we will resolve it.

7. Refunds

All transactions are final once access has been granted, unless a specific refund policy applies or the law requires otherwise. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account termination.

8. Creator payouts

Creators are paid to the bank account or mobile money number set in Payout method. Verified tax information may be required before payouts are released.

9. Contact

Questions about a payment? Contact us at customer@lipaidox.com
