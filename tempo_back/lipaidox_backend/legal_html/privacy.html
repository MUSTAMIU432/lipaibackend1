  • All Lipaiddox users (creators and consumers)

1. Where We Transfer Data Your data may be transferred to and processed in:

8. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

1. Encryption

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Payment information is encrypted and tokenized

2. Access Controls

  • Role-based access control (RBAC) limits employee access to personal data
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for administrative access
  • Regular access reviews and revocation procedures

3. Monitoring and Auditing

  • Continuous security monitoring and intrusion detection
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Append-only audit logs for all data access

4. Data Minimization and Pseudonymization

  • We collect only the data necessary for the purposes described
  • Personal data is pseudonymized where possible for analytics

5. Employee Training

  • All employees receive regular data protection and security training
  • Confidentiality obligations are contractually binding

6. Incident Response

  • We maintain an incident response plan for data breaches
  • Regular testing of our response procedures

7. Third-Party Security

  • All third-party processors are required to maintain equivalent security measures
  • Regular security assessments of third-party vendors

10. YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS.

Under applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and South Africa POPIA, you have the following rights:

1. Right to Access (Article 15 GDPR) You have the right to obtain confirmation from us as to whether or not we process your personal data, and, where that is the case, access to the personal data and certain related information.

2. Right to Rectification (Article 16 GDPR) You have the right to obtain from us the rectification of inaccurate personal data concerning you. You also have the right to have incomplete personal data completed.

3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") (Article 17 GDPR) You have the right to obtain from us the erasure of personal data concerning you without undue delay where one of the specified grounds applies, including where the data is no longer necessary, you withdraw consent, or you object to processing.

4. Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18 GDPR) You have the right to obtain from us the restriction of processing where you contest the accuracy of the data, the processing is unlawful but you oppose erasure, or we no longer need the data but you require it for legal claims.

5. Right to Data Portability (Article 20 GDPR) You have the right to receive the personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and have the right to transmit that data to another controller.

6. Right to Object (Article 21 GDPR) You have the right to object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing of personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

7. Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7(3) GDPR) Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

8. Right to Lodge a Complaint You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Tanzania Data Protection Commissioner, the Kenya Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or the South Africa Information Regulator.

How to Exercise Your Rights You may exercise your rights by:

  • Visiting Settings → Privacy → Data Rights in the Lipaiddox app
  • Emailing us at privacy@lipaiddox.com
  • Writing to us at our physical address

We will respond to your request within 30 days as required by law. We may extend this period by up to 60 days for complex requests, in which case we will inform you of the extension within 30 days.

11. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING AND PROFILING

We use automated processing and profiling to provide personalized experiences on Lipaiddox. This includes:

1. Content Recommendation Algorithms We use automated systems to analyze your interactions, preferences, and behavior to recommend content, creators, and products that may interest you. These recommendations are based on factors such as:

  • Content you have viewed, liked, or shared
  • Creators you follow or subscribe to
  • Your engagement patterns and time spent on content
  • Your location and language preferences

2. Advertising Personalization We use automated systems to select and display advertisements that are relevant to your interests based on your activity on and off Lipaiddox.

3. Fraud Detection and Security We use automated systems to detect and prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and security threats. This includes analyzing patterns of behavior to identify suspicious accounts.

Your Rights Regarding Automated Decisions You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

If we make automated decisions that have legal or significant effects, you have the right to:

  • Obtain human intervention
  • Express your point of view
  • Contest the decision

You can opt out of personalized recommendations and advertising by adjusting your settings in Settings → Privacy → Personalization.

12. DATA PROTECTION IMPACT ASSESSMENTS

Where processing is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments in accordance with applicable law.

1. When We Conduct DPIAs We conduct DPIAs for:

  • New processing activities that involve high-risk data
  • Significant changes to existing processing
  • Processing involving sensitive personal data
  • Processing using new technologies
  • Processing that involves systematic monitoring or profiling

2. DPIA Process Our DPIA process includes:

  • Description of the processing and its purposes
  • Assessment of necessity and proportionality
  • Identification and assessment of risks
  • Identification of measures to address risks

3. Consultation Where required, we consult with supervisory authorities before commencing high-risk processing.

13. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

We take data security seriously and have procedures in place to detect, report, and investigate personal data breaches.

1. Detection and Assessment Upon becoming aware of a breach, we will:

  • Immediately investigate the scope and impact
  • Assess the risk to affected individuals
  • Take steps to contain and remediate the breach

2. Notification to Regulatory Authorities Where required by law, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority:

  • GDPR: Within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Kenya DPA: Without undue delay
  • Nigeria NDPA: Within 72 hours
  • South Africa POPIA: As soon as reasonably possible

3. Notification to Affected Individuals Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you without undue delay, providing:

  • Description of the nature of the breach
  • Contact details of our Data Protection Officer
  • Likely consequences of the breach
  • Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

4. Record Keeping We maintain a register of all personal data breaches, including the facts surrounding the breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken.
