Creator Program Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The rules for earning on Lipaidox as a creator.

1. Becoming a creator

Any account holder aged 16 or over can switch to a creator account. To sell content you need a paid creator plan and must complete the eligibility requirements shown in Settings, including payment setup and tax information.

2. Plans and fees

Basic, Go Plus and Premium plans let you monetize, with platform fees of 30%, 25% and 20% of your earnings respectively. Higher plans add features such as live credits and subscriber tiers. The Free plan cannot sell content.

3. Free and paid content

Your first three published contents may be free. After that, premium content must be offered as a one-time purchase, a rental or subscriber-only content.

4. Your content, your responsibility

You must own or have the rights to everything you publish, and it must follow our Community Engagement Guidelines. Paid content must match what its title and description promise.

5. Payouts

Earnings are paid to your payout method after fees. We may hold payouts while we verify your tax information or investigate suspected fraud or chargebacks.

6. Enforcement

Breaking these rules can lead to content removal, loss of monetization, withheld earnings from fraudulent sales, or account suspension.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact us at customer@lipaidox.com
