Community Engagement Guidelines

Last updated: September 2026

Lipaidox works when people feel safe to create, ask and share. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, messages, live streams, questions and polls.

1. Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech.
  • Disagree with ideas, not people.
  • Respect people's privacy — don't share someone's personal information without their consent.

2. Post only what you have the right to

Only publish content you created or have permission to use. Don't upload other people's paid content, music or videos without their rights.

3. Never allowed

  • Illegal content of any kind.
  • Anything involving minors in a sexual or harmful way.
  • Intimate images shared without consent.
  • Content that promotes violence or self-harm.

4. Keep it honest

  • No scams, fake giveaways or misleading offers.
  • No spam, fake accounts or bought engagement.
  • In World Ask, keep questions and polls genuine and don't manipulate votes.

5. Live streams

The same rules apply live. Creators are responsible for what happens in their stream, including the chat they allow.

6. Reporting

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, use Report on the post or in the conversation. You can also block anyone who messages you.

7. What happens when rules are broken

We may remove content, limit features, or suspend or close accounts, depending on how serious the issue is and whether it has happened before.
